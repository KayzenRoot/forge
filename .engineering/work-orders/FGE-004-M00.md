### NO-PLACEHOLDER COMPLETENESS RULE
A required M00 capability/technology is not implemented merely because a crate, type, trait, endpoint, schema, feature flag, TODO or documentation entry exists. For admission to module certification it must have:
1. an explicit public/internal contract appropriate to its boundary;
2. executable behavior covering its M00 responsibility;
3. at least one positive proof and one relevant negative/failure proof;
4. integration into the owning authority path where required;
5. no unconditional `todo!`, `unimplemented!`, panic stub, fake success, hard-coded proof verdict or dead facade on the certified path.

Deliberate future-extension hooks are allowed only outside mandatory M00 behavior and must be clearly identified as non-authoritative.
