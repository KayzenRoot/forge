# Requirements

## Foundation requirements
- FR-001: repository SHALL use GEF governance from inception.
- FR-002: repository SHALL expose a documented HIVE integration contract.
- FR-003: all implementation SHALL be driven by bounded Work Orders and Context Locks.
- FR-004: exact-head evidence SHALL precede checkpoint promotion.
- FR-005: HIVE unavailability SHALL not corrupt or invalidate Git-canonical project truth.
- FR-006: architecture SHALL support future web, API, Web3/dApp, game, AI and multi-target product construction without prematurely implementing every target.

Detailed product requirements remain intentionally pending discovery and ADR approval.
